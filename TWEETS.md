**Tweet 1 — what it is**

> Most allowlists work by publicly checking your wallet against a list — which means your whole transaction history is now linked to whatever group you just joined.
>
> Gatecheck proves you're on an allowlist without revealing which entry is yours, your wallet, or the list itself.
>
> Built on @MidnightNtwrk 🌘

---

**Tweet 2 — technical insight**

> The core idea: the issuer publishes one 32-byte Merkle root. That's it — the whole list stays off-chain.
>
> You prove your secret's leaf sits under that root, entirely in your own browser. A one-time nullifier stops replay, without ever linking two check-ins to the same person.
>
> No identity ever touches the chain — just "valid member, not yet used."
